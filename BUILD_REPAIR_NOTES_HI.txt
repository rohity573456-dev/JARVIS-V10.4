Manual QA checklist:
- First launch: microphone permission prompt appears.
- Deny permission: clear explanation appears; allow from Android App Info and retry.
- Tap to Speak: speak a short English command; verify text appears in conversation.
- Tap to Speak: speak a Hindi command; if English recognition fails, Hindi locale retry runs.
- Wake listening: keep app open, say "Jarvis", then say a command.
- Wake listening: say "Jarvis, open YouTube".
- Type "search weather" or another query in search field and press RUN.
- Check Gemini/Groq keys and optional LiteRT-LM model separately.
